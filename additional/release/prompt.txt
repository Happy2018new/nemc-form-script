Original

我需要一个 Minecraft 像素化图片，
它不应该被看出是 AI 生成的。

该图片以附魔魔咒为风格，
并且周围有一些魔法符文，
图片的中央是打开着的附魔台的书，
书应该是浮空的，
并且书应该是对称的。

书的旁边有一个纤细的羽毛。
魔法书需要被魔力光束照耀。
图片应该尽可能简洁。



Translate

I need a pixelated image of Minecraft,
which should not be recognizable as an AI-generated work.

The image should have a style reminiscent of an enchantment spell,
and there should be some magical runes around it.

In the center of the image is an open book of enchantments,
and the book should be floating and symmetrical.

There should be a slender feather beside the book.
The magic book needs to be illuminated by a magical beam of light.
The image should be as simple as possible.
